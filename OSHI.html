TRAILER

STORY

OSHI is an experimental and symbolic short film that portrays the experience of a child with autism facing anxiety and sensory overload. Through a small bear living in a white world that gradually transforms into chaos, the story conveys the vital need for empathy and acceptance.

FINAL IMAGES

DEVELOPMENT

As the sole creator of the project, I was responsible for the entire creative process. I developed the original story and designed the storyboard, establishing a unique aesthetic inspired by anime. To bring this vision to life, I combined 3D and 2D animation techniques, crafting a visual language that balances technical precision with deep emotional resonance.

OSHI in essence, is an invitation to see the world with a new perspective.
