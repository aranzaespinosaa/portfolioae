NINE LIVES

Editorial design merged with Augmented Reality. An edition of the short story "Nine Lives" by Ursula K. Le Guin.

The images were created with the objective of being scanned and viewed through Augmented Reality. This approach aims to provide a more inclusive experience within the story.

SOFTWARES: Blender, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe InDesign.
