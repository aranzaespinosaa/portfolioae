SCOPE is a comprehensive platform designed to streamline the cinematic experience. It bridges the gap between logistical efficiency and community engagement for movie lovers.
Smart Aggregation: An intuitive search engine that compares real-time pricing and seating across all local theaters, eliminating the need for multi-site browsing.
AI Personalization: Advanced algorithms learn user preferences to provide tailored movie recommendations and "where to stream" alerts for titles no longer in theaters.
Social Ecosystem: A dedicated space for cinephiles to build custom lists, rate films, and connect with other enthusiasts. Gamified Experience: Includes a personalized "Cinema Map" to track visited locations and explore new ones, alongside community-driven theater rankings.
